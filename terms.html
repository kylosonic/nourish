2. What Nourish is

Nourish is a general wellness and nutrition tracking application. It estimates the nutrition of meals you log and tracks progress against targets you set. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

3. Estimates are estimates

Recognised foods, portion sizes and the resulting nutrition figures are estimates based on what you or the camera provide. Calorie and macronutrient arithmetic is deterministic and derived from published food composition values, but the inputs carry uncertainty and figures are shown with a “~” where that is the case. Do not rely on Nourish for medical or clinical decisions.

4. Food data

Nutrition values come from the Ethiopian Food Composition Table 2025 published by the Ethiopian Public Health Institute (EPHI) and the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO). Portion weights such as “1 cup = 240 g” are Nourish standard measures, not published composition data, and are labelled accordingly. If you believe a value is wrong, contact data@nourish.app with the dish and the source you are comparing against.
