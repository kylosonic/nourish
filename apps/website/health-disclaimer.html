Health & Nutrition Disclaimer

Version 1.0 · Last updated 19 September 2026

1. Not medical advice

Nourish is a general wellness and nutrition tracking application. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and nothing it shows should be treated as a substitute for consultation with a qualified health professional.

2. Figures are estimates

Food identification, portion sizes and the resulting calorie and macronutrient figures are estimates. Portions you or the camera estimate carry uncertainty, and estimated values are displayed with a “~”. The arithmetic that converts a portion into nutrition is deterministic and uses published composition values, but a precise input cannot be guaranteed.

3. Where the numbers come from

Composition values are taken from the Ethiopian Food Composition Table 2025 (EPHI & FAO). Values the publication leaves blank are left blank in Nourish — they are never filled in with a guess. Portion gram weights are Nourish standard measures and are labelled as such.

4. Safety limits in the app

Nourish applies deterministic safety controls rather than letting a model decide: it enforces a minimum age, refuses calorie targets below a configured safe floor, and validates body inputs against plausible ranges. When your inputs suggest a situation that needs professional input, the app is designed to say so rather than to keep optimising a number.

5. When to seek care

Speak to a qualified health professional before changing your diet if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, managing diabetes or another chronic condition, taking medication that affects appetite or metabolism, or recovering from an eating disorder. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services.

Nourish deliberately does not offer extremely low calorie targets, and it does not provide diagnosis or treatment for any condition.
