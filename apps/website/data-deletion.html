Data Deletion

Version 1.0 · Last updated 19 September 2026

1. Delete everything held on your device

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Nourish removes your profile answers, targets and their history, your meal log (with its nutrition snapshots), your water and weight entries, and the offline food catalogue. Nothing remains on the device afterwards.

2. Delete an account

Accounts exist, but in-app account deletion is not built yet. The deletion flow in the app will explain, before you confirm, exactly what will be removed and what the app keeps. [in-app deletion availability — to be supplied]

Until then, send a deletion request to privacy@nourish.app from the address associated with the account, or with enough detail for us to identify it.

3. What deletion removes

  • Your account record and credentials.
  • Profile, targets, meals, water and weight records held on our servers.
  • Sessions and tokens, which are revoked immediately.

4. What is not held in the first place

Meal photographs sent for analysis are not stored by our service, so there is no image archive to delete. Analysis records hold no account identifier.

5. What may be retained, and why

  • Anonymous correction records (the model's prediction, the food you chose, portion, model and prompt version, timestamp) contain no identifier and cannot be traced back to you after deletion. They are removed by the standard 30-day purge.
  • Infrastructure logs held by our hosting provider may persist for their standard security retention period. [log retention period — to be supplied]
  • Records we are legally required to keep (for example, payment and tax records once paid plans exist) are retained for the period the law requires and nothing else.

6. How long it takes

Deletion requests are actioned within 30 days, and we confirm when it is done. Backups are purged on their normal rotation, which completes within 90 days.
