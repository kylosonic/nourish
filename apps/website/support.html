Support & Account Deletion

Last updated 29 October 2026

Get help

Email support@nourish.app for account access, billing, food-data, privacy, or accessibility support. We aim to respond within two business days.

Delete your account

The fastest route is inside the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Type DELETE to confirm. This permanently removes your account, sessions, consent records, meals, water, and weight history.

If you cannot access the app, submit the request below. Enter the email or Ethiopian mobile number associated with the account. Support may ask you to verify ownership before deleting anything.

What happens next

Verified deletion requests are processed within 30 days and the requester is notified when complete. Anonymous correction records and infrastructure logs follow the retention periods in our Privacy Policy.
