1. Who we are

Nourish is a nutrition tracking application. The data controller is [legal entity name — to be supplied], [registered address — to be supplied]. Privacy contact: privacy@nourish.app.

2. What we treat as sensitive

Your nutrition log, body metrics (age, sex, height, weight, goal, activity level) and meal photographs are health-related information. We treat them as sensitive by default, regardless of the legal threshold that applies where you live.

3. What the app stores on your device

  • Your onboarding answers, daily targets and their history.
  • Your meal log, including the nutrition values recorded at the time you saved each meal.
  • Water and weight entries.
  • A local copy of the Ethiopian food catalogue, so search works offline.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not readable by other apps.

4. What leaves your device

  • Meal analysis. When you ask Nourish to analyse a photo or a description, that content is sent to our analysis service, which forwards it to the configured AI provider to identify the foods.
  • Food catalogue updates. The app downloads the catalogue and its version. No personal data is included in that request.
  • Release metadata. The app checks whether a newer version exists. That request contains no personal data.
  • Sign-in. When you sign in, the mobile number you type is sent to our account service, which normalises it and sends a one-time code by SMS. Nourish collects no name, no email address and no password anywhere.
  • Backup. When you press “Back up now”, the meals, water and weight entries you have logged are copied to your account so they are not lost with the device. That is account-linked health data, and it is sent only when you ask for it — the app never uploads in the background. Restoring a backup onto a new device is not built yet, and the app says so on the account screen.

5. Meal photos are not retained

Images sent for analysis are held in memory only for the duration of that request. Our service does not write them to disk and has no database column for them, so there is no retention period to state: nothing is kept. The app keeps your meal record — the foods, portions and nutrition — not the photograph.

Before upload, the app downscales the image and re-encodes it, which removes exchangeable metadata such as GPS location, device model and capture timestamp.

6. AI improvement is opt-in and off by default

Nourish records which corrections you make (for example, the model proposed “Shiro” and you chose “Misir Wot”) so food recognition can be measured and improved. Those records contain the prediction, your choice, the portion, the model and prompt versions and a timestamp. They contain no account identifier and no image.

Your photographs and corrections are never used to train models unless you explicitly enable “Help improve food recognition” in the app. If you later disable it, future data stops being used.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send your nutrition values to third-party advertising or analytics networks.
  • We do not share your data with other users; there are no social features.
  • We do not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

8. Retention

  • Analysis records (foods detected, confidence, corrections) are kept for 30 days by default and then deleted by a scheduled purge.
  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data, once accounts exist, is kept while your account is open.

9. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and its data. In-app controls are provided for export and deletion; where they are not yet available, write to privacy@nourish.app. See Data Deletion for what is removed and how long it takes.

10. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Analysis endpoints are rate limited and the service stores no user identity for them. Secrets live in environment configuration and are never committed to the source repository.

11. Children

Nourish is not intended for people under 18. The app enforces a minimum age during onboarding and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

12. Changes

When this policy changes materially we will state what changed and when, and ask for consent again where the law requires it.
