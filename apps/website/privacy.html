Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Last updated 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Nourish is a nutrition tracking application operated by the Nourish team in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Privacy and data-protection contact: privacy@nourish.app.

2. What we treat as sensitive

Your nutrition log, body metrics (age, sex, height, weight, goal, activity level), meal photographs, period dates and private symptom entries are health-related information. We treat them as sensitive by default, regardless of the legal threshold that applies where you live.

3. What the app stores on your device

  • Your onboarding answers, daily targets and their history.
  • Your meal log, including the nutrition values recorded at the time you saved each meal.
  • Water and weight entries.
  • Private period entries, cycle settings, symptom selections and daily care check-ins, if you choose to enable cycle tracking.
  • A local copy of the Ethiopian food catalogue, so search works offline.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not readable by other apps.

4. What leaves your device

  • Meal analysis. When you ask Nourish to analyse a photo or a description, that content is sent to our analysis service, which forwards it to the configured AI provider to identify the foods.
  • Food catalogue updates. The app downloads the catalogue and its version. No personal data is included in that request.
  • Release metadata. The app checks whether a newer version exists. That request contains no personal data.
  • Sign-in. You may sign in with email and password, a one-time code sent to your email or phone, or Google. Authentication providers receive the identifier and verification data needed to establish your account.
  • Account sync. Logged meals, water, weight entries, private cycle records and care check-ins are copied to your account when you sync and may sync automatically after reconnect, app foregrounding, or an operating-system background task. A fresh installation can restore this account-linked health data. You can disable account use by signing out and deleting local data.

5. Meal photos are not retained

Images sent for analysis are held in memory only for the duration of that request. Our service does not write them to disk and has no database column for them, so there is no retention period to state: nothing is kept. The app keeps your meal record — the foods, portions and nutrition — not the photograph.

Before upload, the app downscales the image and re-encodes it, which removes exchangeable metadata such as GPS location, device model and capture timestamp.

6. AI improvement is opt-in and off by default

Nourish records which corrections you make (for example, the model proposed “Shiro” and you chose “Misir Wot”) so food recognition can be measured and improved. Those records contain the prediction, your choice, the portion, the model and prompt versions and a timestamp. They contain no account identifier and no image.

Your photographs and corrections are never used to train models unless you explicitly enable “Help improve food recognition” in the app. If you later disable it, future data stops being used.

7. Couple wellness and cycle sharing

Cycle predictions are arithmetic estimates generated from the dates you record. AI is not used to calculate predictions or reach health conclusions. Estimates can be wrong, particularly when cycle lengths vary, and are not contraception, a fertility test, a diagnosis or medical advice.

You may pair with one partner using a single-use code that expires after 24 hours. Sharing is off by default. You control cycle timing, daily check-ins and food-logging streak dates separately, and either partner can revoke any category or disconnect immediately.

When cycle sharing is enabled, your partner receives period start dates and your configured cycle and period lengths. When check-in sharing is enabled, they receive check-in dates. When streak sharing is enabled, they receive only dates on which a meal was logged. A partner never receives raw symptoms, period end dates, meal contents, nutrition values, email or phone identifiers. Our backend does not store pairing codes in readable form.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send your nutrition values to third-party advertising or analytics networks.
  • We do not share health data with another user unless you pair and turn on the specific sharing category described above.
  • We do not use AI to calculate cycle predictions or draw health conclusions.
  • We do not provide medical advice or diagnosis.

9. Retention

  • Analysis records (foods detected, confidence, corrections) are kept for 30 days by default and then deleted by a scheduled purge.
  • Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account data is kept while your account is open and removed when you delete it.

10. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and its data. In-app controls provide local export, local erasure, and permanent account deletion. You can also use the support form or write to privacy@nourish.app. See Data Deletion for what is removed and how long it takes.

11. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Analysis endpoints are rate limited and the service stores no user identity for them. On supported native platforms, the app's local data snapshot is encrypted with a key held in the operating system's secure storage. Secrets live in environment configuration and are never committed to the source repository.

12. Children

Nourish is not intended for people under 18. The app enforces a minimum age during onboarding and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

13. Changes

When this policy changes materially we will state what changed and when, and ask for consent again where the law requires it.
