GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Short answers, no fine print games.

Is the calorie count exact?

No. Portions can be estimates. The arithmetic from the published food values is deterministic, and estimated figures are marked with “~”.

Which foods are covered?

The condensed Ethiopian Food Composition Table 2025: 722 published rows from EPHI and FAO, plus Nourish portion measures.

What happens to my meal photos?

They are sent for analysis and are not stored by the service. Nourish keeps the meal record, not the photo.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Search, food logging, hydration, and history work from the on-device catalogue.

Can I install it on iPhone?

Not yet. iOS distribution needs Apple signing. The unsigned test IPA is not installable and is not presented as a download.
